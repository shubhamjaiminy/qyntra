# Qyntra design-partner agreement

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction
> review it once before first use, then fill in the brackets per
> partner. It's deliberately one page: a design partner should be able
> to sign it without a procurement process.

**Between** [Qyntra legal entity], [address] ("Qyntra"), and
[Customer legal name], [address] ("Customer").
**Start date:** [date]

1. **What Qyntra provides.** Use of the Qyntra CLI in Customer's own
   environment, and the Design partner plan on Qyntra Cloud
   ([cloud URL]): up to 10 projects, 50 members and 20,000 runs a
   month, with 13 months of history. Also a weekly review of results,
   and support by [email/Slack] on business days.

2. **Fee.** Free for the first 14 days from the start date. After that,
   USD 500 per month, excluding taxes, billed monthly in advance
   [by card through Qyntra Cloud / by invoice, payable within 14 days].
   The fee is fixed for 12 months from the first paid month.

3. **Guarantee.** If, in the first paid month, Qyntra does not average
   either at least one confirmed bug caught or three confirmed false
   alarms avoided per week, with at least 80% of its claims confirmed
   by Customer (as recorded in the weekly scorecard), Qyntra refunds
   that month's fee on request.

4. **Term and cancellation.** Month to month. Either party may cancel
   at any time with effect from the end of the current paid month. No
   fee is charged after cancellation.

5. **Customer's data.** Qyntra runs in Customer's environment. Qyntra
   Cloud receives only the run summaries Customer's configuration sends,
   at the upload level Customer sets (metrics, standard or full), and
   never source code, screenshots, traces, page content or request
   bodies. Calls to an AI provider go to the provider Customer
   configures, under Customer's own account. Qyntra uses Customer's data
   only to provide the service, and deletes it within 30 days of a
   written request or the end of this agreement.

6. **Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's non-public
   information confidential, uses it only for this agreement, and
   protects it with reasonable care. This survives for 2 years after
   the agreement ends.

7. **Feedback.** Qyntra may use Customer's product feedback to improve
   Qyntra, without identifying Customer.

8. **Publicity.** Qyntra may name Customer, or use Customer's logo or
   quotes, only with Customer's prior written approval, given case by
   case.

9. **Pre-release software.** Qyntra is under active development and is
   provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. Customer decides
   what to release. Qyntra's verdicts are advice to Customer, not a
   guarantee.

10. **Liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential
    losses. Each party's total liability under this agreement is
    limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

11. **Governing law.** [Jurisdiction].
